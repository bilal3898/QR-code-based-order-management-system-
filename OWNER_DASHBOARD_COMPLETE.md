## 🚀 **How to Access Owner Dashboard:**

### **Step 1: Register Owner Account**
1. **Visit:** `http://localhost:3000/owner/register`
2. **Fill Form:** Username, email, password, phone
3. **Submit:** Account created successfully

### **Step 2: Login to Owner Dashboard**
1. **Visit:** `http://localhost:3000/login` (unified) or `http://localhost:3000/owner/login`
2. **Select:** Owner tab
3. **Enter:** Email + password
4. **Submit:** Auto-redirect to owner dashboard

### **Step 3: Manage Restaurant**
1. **Dashboard:** View overview and statistics
2. **Staff:** Manage employees
3. **Menu:** Control menu items and pricing
4. **Tables:** Manage table layout and QR codes
5. **Reports:** View analytics and insights
6. **Inventory:** Track stock and supplies
